2026 · Banking · Operational risk

Employee attendance and block leave anomaly dashboard

Operational risk teams needed a reliable way to spot unusual attendance and leave records across thousands of employees. I built a Power BI dashboard that flags long unexplained absences, tracks the trend over three months and breaks anomalies down by work unit, region and job level.

I cleaned and validated the data with Python and Power Query, reconciled my results against my mentor's analysis, and presented the findings to internal stakeholders.

  • Power BI
  • Power Query
  • Python
  • Pandas
